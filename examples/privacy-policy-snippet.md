Add this section to your privacy policy when using AIPay.

## Content Delivery Network and Security Services (Cloudflare)

### Purpose of Processing

We use Cloudflare, Inc. as our Content Delivery Network (CDN) and security provider. Cloudflare acts as a reverse proxy between your browser and our web server. All requests to our website are routed through Cloudflare's network.

This serves to deliver our website faster and more securely. Cloudflare provides protection against DDoS attacks, unauthorized automated access by bots and AI crawlers, and optimization of loading times through caching.

**AI Access Control:** To protect our content from unauthorized automated scraping by AI systems, we employ an AI access barrier based on Cloudflare. Automated access by AI crawlers is detected and - if not authorized - blocked. Regular human visitors are not affected and can use the website without restrictions.

### Data Processed

When using Cloudflare, the following data may be processed: IP address, requested URL, referrer URL, browser type and version, operating system, date and time of access, and technical connection data. Cloudflare may set performance and security cookies that are technically necessary and do not create personal user profiles.

### Legal Basis

Processing is based on our legitimate interest pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the secure and performant delivery of our website, protection against cyber attacks, and protection of our content from unauthorized automated scraping.

### Third-Country Transfer

Cloudflare is a US company. Data transfer to the USA is based on EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) pursuant to Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR. Cloudflare is also certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. For more information, see Cloudflare's privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

### Storage Duration

According to Cloudflare, log data is typically not stored for longer than 24 hours. Security-related data may be retained for a longer period if necessary to prevent attacks.
